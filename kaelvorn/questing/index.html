No Forgettable Errands. Every Zone Is a War Front.

In Kaelvorn, questing goes far beyond fetching pelts for forgettable villagers. Every region tells a cohesive, high-stakes chapter of the war against the Pale Wake, blending a solo-friendly personal chronicle with massive, real-time public campaigns.

Three Ways to Write Your Chronicle

Experience a fully voiced main storyline designed to be completed solo—with Story-mode dungeons supported by Wayfarer mercenaries—that adapts to your chosen Faction and Calling. Negotiate tense alliances between Bastion knights, Syndicate engineers, and Chorus wardens, investigate why the Founders' signet-tones unlocked Sol-Vara from within, and choose how reclaimed Chime-Spires are re-tuned.

Regional stories play out in real time across the open world. Help a Syndicate armored train fight through five miles of Corsair ambushes in the Cinder-Reach, rally thirty players to defend the Boreal Pipes from a Vitrified siege in the Frost-Chime Peaks, or escort a Chorus Leviathan-turtle through a crystal-choked swamp. If a town falls silent, players must retake it and ring its town bell to restore its services.

Step inside any neutral Wayfarer Lantern tavern to pick up investigative side-stories, monster hunts with acoustic tracking mechanics, and multi-part Architect relic expeditions that carry you across all six regions.

    A solo campaign, a shared world

    In Kaelvorn, questing goes far beyond fetching pelts for forgettable villagers. Every zone tells a cohesive, high-stakes chapter of the war against the Pale Wake, blending cinematic personal storytelling with large-scale public campaigns.

    The Oath-Bearer campaign is solo. Story Toll takes 1–3 players, with Wayfarer mercenaries filling the party.

    Questions reserved for beyond launch

    • Joren Vos and High Commander Veyra: whether Joren Vos or High Commander Veyra can be thawed
    • Founders and Architects: whether the Founders’ signet-tones opened Sol-Vara because the Architects meant them to
    • The First Spire to Fail: which of the three spires fails first

    As players push deeper into the storm, a haunting question emerges: the architectural seals inside Sol-Vara were not broken from the outside, nor did they fail from age. They were unlocked from within using the royal signet-tones of Kaelvorn’s long-vanished Founders. Even more unsettling, the geometric patterns of the Vitrified legions match the sacred carvings inside the temples of the Dawn-Vow, suggesting the Chime-Spires may never have been built to protect life on Kaelvorn—they may have been built to ripen the continent for the Stilling all along.
