Kaelvorn · Field codex

No Single Note Can Make a Chord

Kaelvorn is built from the ground up to celebrate camaraderie, shared pride, and frictionless cooperative play. All guilds, parties, dungeons, and raids are 100% cross-faction. Choosing the Solaris Bastion, the Iron-Chime Syndicate, or the Verdant Chorus shapes your personal story campaign, dyes your war-camp quarter in Last Accord, and unlocks that faction’s mount quartermaster—it never walls you off from your friends. There is no open-world faction ganking; planned post-launch rivalry lives in the seasonal Race to the Spire and the dueling floor of the Mirage-Bazaar.

Bell-Keeps, Wayfarer Lofts & Tavern Life

Every guild can claim and customize a Bell-Keep—an instanced island stronghold suspended over the Mirror-Reach. Together, guild members upgrade its central Chime-Tower, build communal Sun-Smithing and Hearth-Keeping workshops, hang trophies taken from raid bosses, and ring the Guild Great-Bell to grant all online members continent-wide combat and crafting buffs for the evening.

Alongside your guild’s island, every Oath-Bearer keeps a personal Wayfarer Loft above a neutral tavern: an instanced room of warm timber and brass with a weapon rack for your favorite skins, an instrument hook, trophy shelves, and a Tuning-Lantern peg.

Drop your custom Sylvan-Woven Guild Banner into the ground anywhere in the open world to create an instant rally point, granting nearby allies—whether in your guild or passing strangers—warmth against the Pale Wake and shared bonus loot.

Inside every major city tavern, form bands using playable in-game brass and string instruments that harmonize automatically, challenge rivals to Chime-Stone (an in-world tactical dice and tile game), or share a 20-player Hearth-Keeping campfire feast before raid night.
