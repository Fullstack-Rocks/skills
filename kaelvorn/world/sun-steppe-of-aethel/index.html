The Sun-Steppe of Aethel

Golden grassland plateaus and white limestone citadels where the ancient resonant roads still hum beneath the hooves of proud knightly orders.

Regional field notes

Levels 1–20

Golden grassland plateaus and white limestone citadels where the ancient resonant roads still hum beneath the hooves of proud knightly orders.

The heartland of the Solaris Bastion is a welcoming yet dramatic expanse of tiered grasslands, terraced olive and sun-grain farms, and towering white limestone cliffs that step down toward the western Churn. Ancient aqueducts of pale stone and bronze carry shimmering golden water from the peaks of Mount Aethel to power village mills and resonant chimes. High Solitude stands proudly upon the marble terraces of old Alabaster Reach.

Though long the safest region on Kaelvorn, the Steppe is now under intense pressure. Deep fissures have cracked open across the southern pastures, releasing aggressive subterranean crystal-boars, while vanguard scouts of the Pale Wake have begun landing on the northern coastal cliffs.

    Landmarks of the Western Highlands

    • Landmark 1 — High Solitude: The breathtaking fortress-capital of the Solaris Bastion, carved into the white limestone steps of Mount Aethel above the terraces of old Alabaster Reach, crowned by five golden bell-towers and watched over by Spire Aethel.
    • Landmark 2 — The Viaduct of Ten Chords: A monumental two-mile-long fortified bridge and aqueduct that carries both the golden waters of Mount Aethel and pilgrim caravans into the capital, defended by the Order of the Uncracked Bell.
    • Landmark 3 — Sunken Oakhaven: A prosperous rural market town recently half-swallowed by a sudden resonant sinkhole, where Wayfarer lanterns now guide rescue parties through tilted streets.
    • Landmark 4 — The Beacon of Dawn-Watch: A towering coastal chime-lighthouse on the northern cliffs where Bastion lookouts watch for Red-Sail Corsair raiders and Vitrified rifts.

      High Solitude

      High Solitude, a breathtaking fortress-city carved into the white limestone steps of Mount Aethel in the western highlands, crowned by five golden bell-towers and bridged by aqueducts of glowing amber water.

      Threats on the Steppe

      Roaming packs of brass-maned Steppe-Lions, outlaw mercenaries deserting the northern front, burrowing Quartz-Tuskers, and isolated Vitrified Rifts dropping glass-forged constructs onto rural farmsteads.

      • Field Dangers: Roaming packs of brass-maned Steppe-Lions, outlaw mercenaries deserting the northern front, burrowing Quartz-Tuskers emerging from southern fissures, and isolated Vitrified Rifts dropping glass constructs onto rural farmsteads.
      • Region Dungeon — The Sunken Bell-Foundry (Level 20): Dive into a recently drained subterranean bell-foundry where rogue Syndicate scavengers and awakened bronze constructs battle over an intact Architect Prime-Mold guarded by Crucible-Master Vorgath.
      • World Boss — The Toll-Lion of Dawn-Watch: A brass-maned apex lion the height of a bell-tower whose roar rings the coastal Beacon and pulls every nearby Chime-Post into the fight.
      • Region Exemplar — Valen Korr: Meet the First Shield of the Viaduct, whose stone-flesh wrist bears the porcelain scar of Sol-Vara.

        The Sunken Bell-Foundry

        Two hundred years ago, this cavernous subterranean foundry cast the replacement bells for the western Chime-Spires—until a sudden underground flood drowned the lower halls. Recently, the water level dropped after a nearby earthquake, revealing that a cell of rogue Syndicate scavengers and awakened bronze constructs are fighting over an intact Architect Prime-Mold at the bottom of the pit. Players must navigate giant waterwheels, redirect rivers of molten bronze to clear flooded passages, and silence malfunctioning sonic hammers.

        Valen Korr — The Bell-Warden

        Ness Quin — The Dawn-Lancer

        A daring sky-cavalry captain of High Solitude who leaps from the backs of feathered coursers with a twelve-foot prism-lance, piercing the storm from above on wings of solid golden light.

        A flamboyant, cane-twirling physician, exiled from Brass-Harbor for testing Dead-Pitch on the wounded, who now brews marsh-spore and Amber-glass fogs for the Bastion tents at Last Accord, hurling restorative mist at his friends and acid-pitch at his foes.
