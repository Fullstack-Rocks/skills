Systems Built for the Long Road

  • Watch the Canopy Above

    Amber-Tides empower dormant Architect ruins and ignite glider updrafts. Silver-Squalls drop Vitrified rifts onto the roads. Ring regional Ward-Bells to push back the Stilling and wake frozen towns.

  • No Forgotten Errands

    Play a fully voiced Oath-Bearer Campaign solo or with friends, defend Syndicate armored trains across five miles of canyon track, and pick up multi-region monster hunts at any Wayfarer Lantern tavern.

  • Claim Your Island in the Reach

    Build a communal Guild Bell-Keep above the inland sea and ring its Great-Bell for continent-wide raid buffs, or retire to your personal Wayfarer Loft above a neutral tavern.

  • From Drained Foundries to Glass Cloisters

    Dive into five launch dungeons with Story mode for 1–3 players (backed by Wayfarer mercenaries) and five endgame Over-Tune ranks at level 60.

  • Ascend the Broken Spire

    Reclaim the three-mile Boreal Wind-Wall in a howling blizzard, then climb the floating rubble chains into Sol-Vara: The Sanctum of Stillness across Story, Heroic, and Mythic tiers.

  • Steeds of Hoof, Claw, and Brass

    Earn every mount and pet through gameplay—from road-skimming Sun-Coursers and hovering Sand-Skimmers to the prestige Vitrified Sky-Seraph dropped on Heroic Avren.

Kaelvorn is an acoustic fantasy MMORPG: thirteen Callings, six regions kept alive by bronze Chime-Spires, and a silver storm turning the continent into singing porcelain.

No accounts, no beta list, nothing to install—just come back.

A Continent Tuned by Sound and Light

In Kaelvorn, light has weight and sound has shape. Every system is built around the living law of Resonance.

The Chime-Spire Grid

Raw daylight falls through the Canopy as heavy Sun-Pitch. Without vibration, it calcifies water and soil into brittle crystal. Explore six vast regions kept supple by the sub-audible music of the Chime-Spires—and carry your Tuning-Lantern when you step beyond their reach.

  • Explore the Continent →

Oath-Bearers of the Unbroken

Your inner resonance cannot be finished by the Pale Wake. Create your own Korvath, Auralin, Brannik, or Human hero and master one of thirteen distinct Callings—from bell-shield sentinels and marsh lantern-weavers to dual-saber desert dervishes and foundry artillerists.

  • Browse the Roster →

Three-Branch Trees & Seven Crafts

Shape your combat rhythm across three specialized branches and choose the single Tier-4 Ultimate capstone that defines your role and transforms your opening strike. Outside combat, seven artisan crafts change how your gear glows and rings. Every Oath-Bearer masters two primary trades alongside all three gathering skills.

  • See the seven crafts

Three Banners, One Chord

Pledge your banner to the Solaris Bastion, the Iron-Chime Syndicate, or the Verdant Chorus for story and cosmetics. All three factions share guilds and raids: gather for Story raids for 10 and Heroic raids for 20.
