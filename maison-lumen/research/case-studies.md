## 2. Analysis of 10 Published Case Studies

### 1. AKQA — LOEWE: *Casa LOEWE* (Luxury Digital Flagship)
* **Source:** https://www.akqa.com/work/loewe/casa-loewe/
* **Structure:** Three-act editorial structure labeled **Insight** ("LOEWE does it differently" — framing the Spanish heritage house, leather craft, and LOEWE FOUNDATION Craft Prize), **Idea** ("An art gallery inspired UX/UI system to honour LOEWE's commitment into art"), and **Impact** ("An infinite online gallery anticipating and empowering all future brand needs"). Challenge and Execution are woven into Insight and Idea rather than separated into rigid sub-headers.
* **Length & Image vs. Text:** ~290 words of case copy (409 words total on page) paired with 11 images, 4 looping video/iframe embeds, and 6 `<figure>` blocks (~80% visual / 20% text).
* **Metrics & Credibility:** Lists three hard commercial metrics at the close: **Sales vs. previous year: +165%**, **Conversion rate: +108%**, **Product exploration per session: +20%**, plus operational scope (**launched within 6 months across 4 languages and 20+ markets**). Credibility is **moderate-to-high**: the operational scope (6 months, 4 languages, 20+ markets) is verifiable, while the percentage lifts (+165% sales, +108% CVR) are strong relative indicators but lack baseline denominators or attribution windows.
* **Client Credit:** Centres Creative Director **Jonathan Anderson** with a direct pull-quote (*"I like classicism meets modernity... I like contradictions that work"*) and credits the house's artisans and foundation before describing AKQA's UX/UI system, concluding with a live link (`loewe.com ↘`).

* **Source:** https://www.akqa.com/work/alaia/a-digital-space-built-with-intention/
* **Structure:** Explicitly labeled sections: **Problem** (translating Azzedine Alaïa's sculptural mastery under Creative Director Pieter Mulier without compromising commerce), **Background**, **Ambition** ("mirrors the intimacy of the boutique"), **IDEA: Sculptural Beauty** (treating negative space as fabric; black, white, and ALAÏA red; silhouette scroll and 360° product views), **Role** (numbered `01` to `08`: Design concept, Design system, Design templates, Module library, Contribution guidelines, Social launch assets, Visual expression, Product experience), and **Result**.

## 3. Synthesis: Patterns Across Successful Luxury Case Studies

1. **The "Insight → Idea → Craft → Proof" Spine Dominates:** Across AKQA ([Casa LOEWE](https://www.akqa.com/work/loewe/casa-loewe/), [Cheval Blanc](https://www.akqa.com/work/cheval-blanc/the-art-of-surprise/)) and Construct London ([Aman](https://constructlondon.com/work/aman), [The Lana](https://constructlondon.com/work/thelana), [Boodles](https://constructlondon.com/work/boodles)), luxury agencies reject verbose agency jargon in favor of a tight 3- or 4-part spine: **Context/Challenge → Insight → Idea/Execution → Impact**.
2. **Word Counts Are Restraint-First (250–650 Words):** None of the 10 case studies exceed 700 words of main body copy. The median body text length is **~420 words**. Visuals occupy **80%–90% of the vertical scroll**, typically mixing 10–35 still frames with 4–15 silent looping motion clips.
3. **What Makes Metrics Credible in Luxury:**
   * **Low credibility / rarely used by top luxury agencies:** Uncontextualized "300% traffic boost" claims or ad-spend ROAS numbers, which feel mass-market and violate luxury discretion.
   * **High credibility in luxury:** (a) **Architectural & compliance metrics** (e.g., Decimal Studios citing automated **WCAG AA compliance** for [Audemars Piguet](https://decimalstudios.com/projects/ap-chronicles); AREA 17 citing a **WCAG AA Storybook library** and **4-month launch** for [Accor](https://area17.com/clients/accor); BASIC/DEPT® citing **static bundle deployment across 15 localized sites** for [Gucci Ancora](https://www.basicagency.com/case-studies/gucci-ancora)), (b) **Dwell time and depth of exploration** (makemepulse citing **10-minute average dwell time** for [AP House of Wonders](https://www.makemepulse.com/case-study/audemars-piguet-the-house-of-wonders); AKQA citing **+20% product exploration per session** for [LOEWE](https://www.akqa.com/work/loewe/casa-loewe/)), and (c) **Enduring longevity & institutional accolades** (SDL's **2017–2025 partnership** with [Polestar](https://www.stockholmdesignlab.se/work/polestar); Boodles' **Walpole British Luxury Brand of the Year** via [Construct London](https://constructlondon.com/work/boodles)).
4. **How Clients Are Credited:** The agency never claims sole authorship of the house's taste. Top case studies name the house's founder, creative director, family stewards, or internal team by name (e.g., Jonathan Anderson at LOEWE, Pieter Mulier at ALAÏA, Sabato De Sarno at Gucci, the Wainwright family at Boodles, and the 5 named Audemars Piguet client leads in Decimal Studios' credit roll).

## 4. Template for a Maison Lumen Case Study Page

Aligned with [BRIEF.md](../BRIEF.md) (*"Quiet luxury. Restraint over spectacle, craft over campaigns, and proof over adjectives... static HTML/CSS/JS, WCAG 2.2 AA"*), each Maison Lumen case study should target **350–450 words total**, **12–16 curated visual plates** (~85% visual / 15% text), and follow this exact 6-section structure:

```markdown
# [House Name] — [One-Line Poetic but Concrete Subtitle, e.g., "The Digital Atelier for a 12-Piece Horology House"]

**Metadata Bar (dl/dt/dd)**
- **House:** [Client Name, Location — e.g., Geneva / Modena / Private Archipelago]
- **Commission:** Brand Story & Voice · Visual Identity · Digital Flagship (`index.html`)
- **Year:** [Year]
- **Standard:** WCAG 2.2 AA Verified · Static Zero-Framework Architecture
- **Flagship:** [Link to built client site: `../../sites/<slug>/index.html`]

---

## 01. The House & The Refusal (Challenge) [~60–75 words]
State who the house is, how rarely its clients buy, and **what the house refuses to do** (e.g., no configurator gimmicks, no public booking engines, no pop-ups, no dark-pattern urgency). Explain the friction between the house's physical craft and a conventional luxury website.

> [Full-bleed Hero Plate: Still image of the flagship homepage in context + descriptive `alt` text]

## 02. The Insight [~60–75 words]
One sharp observation from studying the house's physical materials, archives, or guest ritual (inspired by Pentagram's Scaglietti swatches for Ferrari or Construct's elemental alphabet for Aman). Include one short pull-quote from the House's founder, master watchmaker, chief engineer, or estate host.

> [2-Column Plate: Left — Physical craft/material detail (leather, hand-bevelled bridge, limestone); Right — Digital translation (colour token, typographic specimen, icon grid)]

## 03. The Digital Flagship (Idea & Execution) [~140–170 words]
Divide into 3 numbered craft notes (01 Identity & Voice, 02 Spatial Rhythm & Interaction, 03 Engineering & Accessibility):
- **01 Identity & Voice:** Colour palette (with exact hex codes and contrast ratios), typography, monogram/icon set, and editorial restraint.
- **02 Spatial Rhythm:** How the page paces discovery before the product/invitation—treating the website as the boutique, not the brochure.
- **03 Engineering & Accessibility:** How the static HTML/CSS architecture achieves sub-second rendering, keyboard focus visibility, real HTML text (zero baked-in image text), and `prefers-reduced-motion` poise.

> [6–8 Visual Plates: Desktop and mobile viewports, typography/monogram sheet, keyboard focus state detail, and interaction states]

## 04. Proof (Results & Verification) [~50–70 words + 4-stat grid]
Replace unverifiable marketing adjectives with a 4-column **Proof Block** combining verified technical craft metrics and commercial/engagement indicators:
- **[Metric 1 — Technical Speed]:** e.g., `0 KB` JS frameworks / `< 0.8s` First Contentful Paint
- **[Metric 2 — Accessibility]:** `100% WCAG 2.2 AA` (min contrast ratio `X.X:1`, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured)
- **[Metric 3 — Engagement Depth]:** e.g., `[X] min` median reading/exploration time or `[X]%` private inquiry / salon appointment completion
- **[Metric 4 — Deliverable Scope]:** e.g., Bespoke monogram, `[X]`-icon set, art direction, and zero-dependency digital flagship

## 05. In Partnership With (Credits) [~30–40 words]
A three-column credit block modelled on Pentagram and Decimal Studios:
- **The House ([Client Name]):** Named founder / creative director / archivist / host
- **Maison Lumen (Paris & Milan):** Brand Strategy, Identity, Art Direction, Interface Engineering
- **Accessibility & UX Review:** Independent audit (`reviews/<slug>.md`)
```

## 5. What Maison Lumen Should Do (8 Concrete, Testable Recommendations)

1. **Cap case study body copy between 350 and 500 words across exactly four narrative headings (`01 The House & The Refusal`, `02 The Insight`, `03 The Digital Flagship`, `04 Proof`).**
   * *Why:* Matches the empirical sweet spot of [AKQA's ALAÏA (430 words)](https://www.akqa.com/work/alaia/a-digital-space-built-with-intention/), [Hypersolid's Polestar (410 words)](https://www.hypersolid.com/work/polestar-partnership), and [SDL's Polestar (480 words)](https://www.stockholmdesignlab.se/work/polestar).
   * *Test:* Word count of `<main>` prose (excluding metadata and credits) is `>= 350` and `<= 500` words.

2. **Maintain an 80:20 visual-to-text vertical scroll ratio using 12–16 static HTML `<figure>` plates with visible `<figcaption>` technical notes.**
   * *Why:* Like [Stockholm Design Lab's Polestar page](https://www.stockholmdesignlab.se/work/polestar) and [Pentagram's Ferrari page](https://www.pentagram.com/work/ferrari-under-the-skin), luxury case studies let the work speak first and use short captions to explain material and typographic decisions.
   * *Test:* Each case study contains 12–16 `<figure>` elements, every `<img>` has a descriptive `alt` attribute (`>= 10` words), and every `<figure>` has a concise `<figcaption>`.

3. **Pair every physical brand cue with its digital counterpart in a dedicated "Material-to-Screen" comparison block.**
   * *Why:* [Pentagram's Ferrari case study](https://www.pentagram.com/work/ferrari-under-the-skin) derives its palette from 1960s Scaglietti swatches and wayfinding from Maranello factory floor markings; [Construct's Aman case study](https://constructlondon.com/work/aman) derives its glyph system from pre-Roman petroglyphs.
   * *Test:* Section `02` of each case study explicitly documents the physical inspiration alongside the exact hex colour tokens (with WCAG contrast ratios) and typeface specimens used in `sites/<slug>/styles.css`.

4. **Publish verifiable engineering and WCAG 2.2 AA metrics alongside behavioural engagement metrics—never uncontextualized ROAS or discount-driven e-commerce stats.**
   * *Why:* Following [Decimal Studios' Audemars Piguet case study](https://decimalstudios.com/projects/ap-chronicles) (WCAG AA + automated scans), [AREA 17's Accor case study](https://area17.com/clients/accor) (AA-compliant component library), and [BASIC/DEPT®'s Gucci Ancora case study](https://www.basicagency.com/case-studies/gucci-ancora) (static bundle speed), technical proof reinforces Maison Lumen's *"proof over adjectives"* rule.
   * *Test:* The `04 Proof` block includes at least two inspectable technical metrics (e.g., measured WCAG contrast ratio, total page weight in KB, zero external JS dependencies) and at least one behavioural/appointment metric (e.g., dwell time or private salon/allocation inquiries).
