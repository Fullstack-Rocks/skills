The atelier

The work on the bench sets the subject.

Tomé conserves furniture here throughout the year, for private owners as well as for the house. Guests see the work already in progress; nothing is staged for a residence.

An atelier morning is optional. You may spend it with Tomé at the bench, looking at the object, its materials and its treatment record. Participation is never expected.

Objects are shown only with their owners’ permission.

Commissioned conservation work is quoted separately, before commitment.
