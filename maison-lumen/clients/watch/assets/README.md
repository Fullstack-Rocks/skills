## Photography delivery

These nine photographs were newly generated via Nano Banana (`gemini-api image` at `2K` with closest supported aspect ratios); no pixels were cropped or upscaled from `design.jpg`. Each prompt was written as a photographer's brief defining subject, setting, light, lens, and the palette and mood from `profile.md` section 7 (tactile Fleurier atelier photography, warm ivory workshop paper `#F3F0E8`, graphite-green shadows `#232824`, movement dark `#171C19`, slate `#535D56`, aged brass `#9D845B`, soft side daylight for complete watches and people, and controlled directional macro lighting for movement finishing), while keeping Alix Talvrenne, Éline Dervaillet, the Fleurier workshop, and the unbranded sterile matte-silver T-01 consistent across the set. All prompts prohibited text, logos, watermarks and real brands.

Generated source images were resized with `sips` to the exact delivery dimensions above and converted using `cwebp -q 82`. Every delivered WebP is under 400 KB, and all intermediate JPEG files were deleted. The SVGs and wordmark PNG were left unchanged.
